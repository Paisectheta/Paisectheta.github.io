## THE EXPERIMENT; STATUS: PERMANENTLY INCOMPLETE / ALWAYS W.I.P

## What the hell is this?
This site itself is Project \#001.

In a world where every, and I mean EVERY, porfolio is supposed to look like a polished, high-converting SaaS landing page designed to impress a hiring manager, this site exists to do *THE EXACT* opposite. Not the impressing part, oh hell no. The part about *"being polished"* is what we're focusing on.

This is a dedicated zone for *me* to mess around, start things without feeling obligated to finish them, and post cool stuff - if I may say so myself - just because and exists AND, it came out MY brain. At least the few braincells that I still have remaining after **gruelling** HOURS of college course work.

So, NO metrics, NO corporate polish, NO "optimizing for conversion." 

Just an old school internet room with the door left unlocked. :D

## The manifesto of unseriousness
This site is built for FUN, not followers. If you stumbled here, AWESOME. Hopefully you get a peek into what I think like and my quirkiness WITHOUT judgement (Honestly? If you judge me, I don't think I can do anything, but then, what can you? You do you)

Like you might have noticed, there is a technical aspect to this website (bloody engineers, always making stuff), and there's and ABSOLUTE messy aspect to this website. Hopefully, I can figure out how to actually make it worthwhile. 

So in conclusion, this is a personal archive. Hobbies, strange rabbit holes I dove into, fun side-quests I finished, lists, etc. You get them HERE. So, explore the site, contact me and maybe, MAYBE, at the end of it all, you feel a little calmer and happier away from (you guessed it), all the noise.
