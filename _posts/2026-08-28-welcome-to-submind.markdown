This is the first real note for the site. The point of the redesign is not nostalgia for its own sake; it is to make the portfolio feel like a place that can hold projects, references, and personal context without turning into a generic landing page.

The new layout keeps navigation at the top, puts page-specific links on the left, and reserves the right rail for updates, quotes, and other useful fragments. That makes the whole site feel more like a small personal wiki than a polished brochure.

## Why this structure works

- The top bar keeps the important sections visible everywhere.
- The left rail gives each page a quick local map.
- The right rail holds recurring context, so every page feels connected.

## What comes next

I’ll keep filling this site with project notes, writing, and other things worth remembering. If a page feels too empty, I’ll treat that as a sign to make it more useful instead of more decorative.
