## What this website is all about
In this age of AI, alot of my work involves relying on AI of some sort of the other to get research / coding done. It's really easy to lose track of your own design philosophies and stuff. And it kind of makes you lose out on alot of learning.

So this site sort of holds me accountable to whatever I do. Hopefully, documenting my work will make me focus more and learn properly and understand all that I do.

So... welcome to this journey!
